Let your assistants know who they're talking to

attest listens to the microphones and cameras you already have, works out who is in the room, and only lets an assistant act for someone in the ways that person has allowed.

  • Uses the accounts you already have. Nobody gets a new password.
  • Never stores voices or faces. Your devices keep those; attest only hears who they think it is.
  • Everyone decides for themselves what an assistant may do for them.

How careful to be

Preset

The four levels

  • Anyone · no need to know who's asking
  • Probably · recognised fairly recently
  • Sure · recognised clearly in the last few minutes
  • Certain · recognised two ways, such as voice and face, just now

Changing a number switches the preset to “Custom”. It takes effect on the next decision; nothing to restart.
